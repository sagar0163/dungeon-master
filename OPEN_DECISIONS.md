# Open Decisions & Considerations

## 1. Product Name & Trademark (Dungeon Lord)

**Context:** 
The Business Requirements Document (BRD) cites the LitRPG series "Dungeon Lord" as an inspiration for this project. Currently, the product and repository share or reference this name.

**Issue:**
There is a potential trademark and branding conflict with the existing "Dungeon Lord" LitRPG series by Hugo Huesca, as well as other potentially trademarked entities in the gaming and literary space. Using the exact same name for a commercial or even a prominent open-source game might lead to confusion or legal pushback.

**Options:**
1. **Keep "Dungeon Lord"**: Accept the risk if the project is purely non-commercial/hobbyist, though it may still face issues if it gains popularity.
2. **Rename the Product**: Brainstorm a new, distinct name that captures the "Dungeon Keeper × Legend of Grimrock" vibe without directly colliding with the LitRPG series (e.g., *Dungeon Sovereign*, *Master of the Depths*, *Grid Lord*).
3. **Reach out for Permission**: Contact the author/rights holder to see if a fan-game or similarly named project is permissible (least likely for a commercial product, but an option for open-source).

**Next Steps:**
- Discuss with stakeholders/project leads.
- Decide on a final product name before any public promotional push or 1.0 release.
